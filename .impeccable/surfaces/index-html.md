# Surface brief: rocodrom.com landing (index.html)

Scope: the single public page at rocodrom.com. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a climber who got the link from a friend at their gym, on a phone. They must understand the game in seconds and join the iOS beta (TestFlight), ideally bringing their group.
Proof: real app screens captured from a backend-free mock build (sample data), the app's real scoring numbers, live Crux.
Constraints: static files on GitHub Pages; 8 languages with auto-detect; calm motion, no bounces; nothing fabricated (no user counts, testimonials, store badges).
Memorable moment: one phone stays pinned while the page changes colour behind it, circuit by circuit, from white to black.
Resolved: the TestFlight link (from the app repo's testflight.md) was confirmed by Marc on 2026-11-18. Unresolved: Android date unknown.
Direction choice: the decision page went unanswered for about an hour, so the build proceeded unattended on the assigned direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one gym circuit. Eight full-bleed colour fields, the app's own default circuits from white to black, each carrying one move of the game (white, green, blue, yellow and orange are the app's exact values; red and purple are lightened one step, #DE5A55 and #9675DE, so ink body text reaches 4.5:1 on them, and black is deepened to #1E2126 so the closing field separates from the app's own dark screens). It refuses the app-landing arrangement of a neutral page with a hero phone, three feature cards and store badges.

OWN-WORLD: Hold-resin colour fields (#F4F6F8, #5FBF7A, #4F8AD9, #E7B75A, #F08A4B, #DE5A55, #9675DE, #1E2126) printed with a single ink (#14171C); chalk white is the only paper. Components are gym objects: laminated route tags with a punched hole, gaffer-tape strips reading SALIDA and TOP, a legend rail of eight circuit squares. Type is a condensed black grotesque at wall scale against small tag labels, with marker handwriting for setter notes. Field edges are cut on a slight diagonal like wall panels. Only the real app screens and Crux keep their own colours.

STORY: Start (set a route, make your friends sweat) → your turn to set → two lives, filming is the attempt → points and flash → the group is the referee → the monthly league → Crux and skins → join the beta.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White field. Legend rail fixed on the left edge with the first square filled. Wordmark top-left. The headline in three uppercase lines at wall scale fills the left two thirds; under it one sentence and the primary action, a solid ink tag "Unirme a la beta" with the platform note beside it. One real phone (home screen with the group cards) stands on the right, tilted, rising from the green field that cuts in diagonally at the bottom. Crux celebrates a top standing on the phone's upper edge (moved there from the wordmark dot during the build: the real home screen already shows a Crux hanging by the wordmark, and one Crux per screen is a brand rule). The SALIDA tape straddles the seam where green cuts in.

FORM: Colour-circuit system of a climbing gym (route tags, tape, hold colours); candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:2fcf7940ca264462. Signature interaction: the pinned phone. It straightens as the hero leaves, stays fixed through seven fields while its screen swaps to the real screen of each move, and the legend fills square by square. Motion grammar: masked line rises, tags that swing to rest, scrubbed panel cuts; exponential ease-out, no bounce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
